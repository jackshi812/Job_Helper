<objective>
Open the closed database unions to Paylocity, admit it to the per-company activation-evidence ledger, and widen the scheduled claim boundary so a promoted Paylocity company is actually polled (D-07, D-09).

Purpose: the database is the second, independent half of every gate in this system; the type union alone grants nothing until the constraints admit the provider. The claim boundary is the last of those gates — a provider that promotes but is never claimed is silently dead, so promotion and claimability land in one migration.
Output: migration `0028_paylocity_connector.sql` and the updated activation-policy assertions.

This migration is authored but **not applied** by this plan. Applying it to the hosted project is plan 11's owner-approved blocking checkpoint. No autonomous task in this phase performs a hosted schema push.
</objective>
