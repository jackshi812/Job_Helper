<objective>
Replace the single fixed Capital One Workday identity with a server-owned reviewed tenant allowlist, and make detection an exact allowlist lookup.

Purpose: the allowlist is the SSRF gate. Host-pattern parsing exists only to produce a candidate triple; it must never widen what is reachable.
Output: `workday-allowlist.ts` holding the eight reviewed entries, and a detection branch that returns unsupported on any allowlist miss.

**Pilot scope is eight companies** (D-01): BMO, Capital One, Avant, Visa, Workday, Apollo Global Management, State Street, Invesco. PIMCO is removed (D-02) and must not be added, guessed, or seeded. Two further companies are outstanding from the owner (D-03) — no artifact, comment, commit message, or test name produced by this plan may describe the pilot as a completed ten-company round.
</objective>
