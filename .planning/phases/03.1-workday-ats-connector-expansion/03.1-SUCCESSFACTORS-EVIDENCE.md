## Why the implementation gate failed

The observed `/services/recruiting/v1/jobs` interface is undocumented as a supported anonymous integration contract. It requires an HTML request to establish a session and extract a CSRF token. Its JSON list responses do not contain the full descriptions needed by the existing preference and scoring pipeline, and the only observed detail responses are HTML.

W. L. Gore additionally produced a duplicate ID across sequential pages during the observation. Statsforvalteren returned zero jobs under the wrong locale. These results make an HTTP 200, a plausible total, or an empty page insufficient evidence of complete and closure-credible enumeration.

SAP's documented `JobRequisition` and `JobRequisitionPosting` OData v2 APIs are permissioned tenant APIs. Job Copilot has no employer credentials or authorization to use those APIs for these employers.

## Explicit non-authorization

This evidence authorizes none of the following:

- DOM or generic HTML parsing
- browser or headless-browser automation
- a `successfactors` detector, adapter, provider enum, normalized-job source, or schema union
- a company or source-coverage catalog row for either employer
- an activation-ledger provider, claim predicate, dispatch entry, or scheduler hook
- host, tenant, locale, company, site, category, or data-center generalization

A future proof may authorize only the exact employer identity it independently proves. That proof must establish a repeatable unauthenticated JSON list-and-detail contract, stable IDs, truthful company identity, reconciled pagination/counts, scoring-quality descriptions, and bounded request behavior. Until then, both employers and the provider remain non-executable.
