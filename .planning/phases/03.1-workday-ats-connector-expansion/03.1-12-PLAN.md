All accepted evidence must be derived server-side by the Edge function from a genuine session — that is the rule Phase 02.1 established and it is what makes the evidence mean anything.

The window guard will reject a second observation inside the same window — that rejection is correct behavior, not an error to work around. | T-03.1-12-03 | Tampering | replayed or same-window evidence inflating progress | high | mitigate | The observation-identifier replay guard, the company-and-window unique constraint, and the three-row cap are enforced in the database; a same-window rejection is recorded as correct behavior |
