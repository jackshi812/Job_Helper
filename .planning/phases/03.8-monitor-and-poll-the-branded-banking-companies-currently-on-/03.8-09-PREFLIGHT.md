# Phase 03.8 selective observation allowlist hotfix

Status: `READY_FOR_OWNER_APPROVAL`

Release 007 deployed successfully and its four direct provider probes passed.
Morgan Stanley then recorded its first production observation, while Bank of
America, BlackRock, and Barclays were rejected before provider fetch because the
experimental Workday allowlist omitted `selectiveRecentUsScope`.

This hotfix adds that single server-owned scope flag to the existing exact
identity gate. Regression coverage proves all four selective identities reach
the adapter while copied and unknown identities remain rejected.
