## Job Scope

| Decision | Options considered | Selected |
|----------|--------------------|----------|
| Geography | U.S. only; all countries with downstream filters; portal-default scope | United States only |
| Categories | No exclusions; broad irrelevant-family exclusions; narrow allowlist; user-defined list | User-defined seven-category allowlist |
| Missing category evidence | Fail closed; title fallback; broad ingest; provider discretion | Fail closed |
| Label matching | Whole-word/phrase; substring; exact label; provider mapping | Case-insensitive whole-word/phrase |

**User's choice:** Ingest only U.S. jobs whose provider-supplied category/job-family label contains Data, Technology, Finance, Investment, Research, Risk, or Capital Markets as a whole word or phrase.

**Notes:** The user was concerned that unfiltered ingestion could overwhelm the system. The agreed safety model combines the narrow category scope with bounded concurrency, staggered polling, time budgets, batched writes, and fail-closed incomplete observations. Title and description inference are not allowed when category evidence is missing.
