# Phase 05.1: Ranking and Dashboard Performance Stabilization - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-07-31
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 05.1 restores the shipped job feed's operational health without mixing in
the paused Phase 5 outreach work. It first stops permanent-dismissal tombstones
from causing repeated full-catalog `new_job` runs, then implements ranking that
is proportional to genuine new or changed jobs and shortens the dismissal
critical path. Historical deployment-provenance reconstruction and ranking-
history retention are not prerequisites for these dashboard fixes.

The phase preserves deterministic ranking and the current feed semantics. Paid
or background AI scoring remains removed. Connector expansion, preference/filter
tuning, outreach work, manual queue repair, `VACUUM FULL`, and unapproved
production cleanup are outside this phase.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Containment Release
- **D-01:** The first production release is containment only: stop dismissal
  tombstones from qualifying as new ranking work. Do not bundle incremental
  ranking, worker batching, retention, or dashboard changes into that release.
- **D-02:** After containment deploys, let existing claimed/building work finish
  or expire naturally. Do not invoke workers manually, cancel runs, or repair
  queue state.
- **D-03 (SUPERSEDED by D-21):** The original pre-redesign 24-hour observation
  is no longer a deployment prerequisite. Preserve its failed evidence as audit
  history; use the simplified current-state release gate and post-release
  monitoring in D-21 instead.
- **D-04:** The containment release deletes no ranking history and performs no
  storage maintenance.
- **D-20:** Containment approval binds only immutable migration, source,
  evidence, target-policy, function-security, isolated-command, exclusion,
  rollback, stash, and authorization-scope identities. Volatile production
  counts, ranking work state, observation timestamps, and fixed-window log
  totals remain informational and are remeasured in a fail-closed gate
  immediately before deployment. Unsafe operational state permits natural
  drain and read-only rechecks only; it does not require new approval unless an
  immutable approved identity or scope changes.

### Ranking Contract
- **D-05:** Deterministic ranking remains the feed publication authority. The
  removed paid AI-scoring path must not be restored.
- **D-06:** Later `new_job` work must scale with genuine new or changed jobs and
  affected owners, never the complete open-job catalog.
- **D-07:** A genuine job must not become visible through a partial ranking
  publication; preserve the existing complete/publication correctness contract
  while removing unrelated full-universe work.

### Dismissal Interaction
- **D-08:** If the dismissal RPC succeeds but feed refill or resume routing later
  fails, keep the job dismissed and show a small retryable refresh warning. Do
  not resurrect durable server state.
- **D-09:** Consider dismissal complete when the dismissal RPC succeeds. Feed
  refill, inactive-cache reconciliation, and resume routing continue outside the
  mutation's user-blocking critical path.
- **D-10:** If the dismissal RPC fails, restore the exact optimistic card at its
  prior position and show a clear retry message.
- **D-11:** While a dismissal RPC is pending, disable lifecycle actions only on
  the targeted card. Other cards remain actionable.

### Ranking-History Retention
- **D-12 (DEFERRED by D-23):** Retain only operationally necessary item history: active/building and
  retry-dependent data plus the latest successful run needed by each owner/run
  reason. Keep seven days of lightweight run summaries, not seven days of full
  item payloads.
- **D-13 (DEFERRED by D-23):** Remove old history only in small monitored batches with abort
  thresholds for lock time, query latency, WAL/replication pressure, and errors.
- **D-14 (DEFERRED by D-23):** After deletion, prefer normal vacuum/analyze and internal page reuse.
  Further index or file-shrink maintenance is a separate capacity decision;
  `VACUUM FULL` is not part of this phase.
- **D-15 (DEFERRED by D-23):** Production deletion requires a separate owner checkpoint showing
  dry-run counts, protected-row invariants, backup/PITR readiness, batch limits,
  and rollback/abort procedures.

### Performance and Acceptance Gates
- **D-16:** Preserve the product freshness target: publication-to-dashboard P95
  within 15 minutes, including database-insert-to-visible P95 within 2 minutes.
- **D-17:** A dismissal row disappears optimistically within 100 ms, unrelated
  cards remain usable, and the targeted mutation settles at P95 within 1.5
  seconds.
- **D-18 (SUPERSEDED by D-21):** The same 24-hour health measurements remain
  required as post-release monitoring, but they no longer block implementation
  or require historical deployment-time identity reconstruction.
- **D-19:** Every `new_job` run must correspond to genuine arrivals; item creation
  must scale as new jobs times affected owners, with no minute-cadence growth
  during the observation window.

### Owner Simplification — 2026-08-01
- **D-21:** Stop Plan 05.1-13 and make its `historical_irrecoverable` and
  `current_baseline_collection_failed` artifacts non-blocking audit history. Do
  not attempt further historical-log or baseline-identity authorization loops.
  Before any later deployment, require only exact source/migration checks,
  targeted local tests, an explicit owner release checkpoint, and simple
  current-state/post-release monitoring that makes no historical identity claim.
- **D-22:** Implement the actual dashboard fixes immediately after completed
  Plan 05.1-03: rank only genuine new or changed jobs instead of the full open
  catalog, batch worker persistence, optimistically remove a dismissed card,
  keep other cards actionable, and move refill/resume-routing work outside the
  dismissal RPC critical path. Implementation and local verification authorize
  no production deployment.
- **D-23:** Defer ranking-history retention, cleanup, vacuum/analyze, and their
  approval machinery to a future phase. They must not block delta ranking or
  fast dismissal, and this simplification authorizes no production deletion.

### the agent's Discretion
- Exact forward-migration structure, delta-selection query shape, batched staging
  API, worker batch size/concurrency, and retry implementation, provided every
  locked release boundary and acceptance gate above remains enforceable.
- Exact inline wording and placement of the retryable post-dismiss refresh
  warning, provided it is accessible and does not block unrelated job actions.
- Exact retention batch size and abort-threshold values, derived conservatively
  from dry-run and hosted read-only measurements before owner approval.

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- Existing deterministic ranking filters and scorer are pure/reproducible and
  should be retained; the defect is work selection and persistence fan-out.
- TanStack Query's existing optimistic snapshot helpers can support exact-card
  rollback and targeted cache replacement without a new state library.
- Existing exact-release and hosted-parity scripts provide patterns for binding
  read-only evidence to a source/deployment identity.

### Established Patterns
- Deployed migrations remain immutable; database repairs are forward-only.
- Partial ranking runs never replace a previously complete published view.
- Production mutations use explicit owner checkpoints, exact scope, restoration
  conditions, and postconditions.
- RLS remains the authorization boundary; performance work must not broaden
  service-role or browser authority.

### Integration Points
- `enqueue_deterministic_new_jobs` must distinguish genuine missing projections
  from permanent-dismissal tombstones and later seed only delta work.
- `stage_deterministic_ranking_result` and the worker hot loop are the set-based
  batching/lock-contention boundary.
- `dismiss_job_permanently`, ranking-item foreign keys, and retention must be
  designed together so user-facing dismissal does not synchronously remove a
  large historical fan-out.
- Dashboard mutation callbacks and `requestDashboardFeedPage` are the frontend
  critical path to shorten without changing feed truth.

</code_context>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Containment must prove that an open dismissed job produces zero enqueue work
  across repeated scheduler ticks while one genuine new job still enqueues.
- Cleanup must not be used to disguise an active producer bug: stop and observe
  the loop first, redesign ranking second, delete history last.
- Production acceptance is based on measured stage timings and cardinality, not
  a subjective claim that the dashboard feels faster.

</specifics>
