## Company Visibility Behavior

| Decision | Options considered | Selected |
|----------|--------------------|----------|
| Preferred-company behavior | Ordering boost; pinned section; preferred-only filter | Preferred-only filter |
| Existing jobs | Hide immediately; hide future only; hide and dismiss | Hide/unhide current-list rows only |
| Persistence | Per-user; session-only; persistent with Reset | Session-only |
| Interaction | Checkbox panel; dropdown; persistent settings list | Checkbox current-list panel |

**User's choice:** A searchable screenshot-style current-list panel that hides/unhides companies only for the session and never filters future ingestion.

## Entry-Level Semantics

| Decision | Options considered | Selected |
|----------|--------------------|----------|
| Filtering approach | Strict; soft scoring; hybrid | Hybrid required-years filter |
| Threshold | Fixed under 3; fixed under 2; configurable | User-configurable maximum |
| Missing years | Keep; hide; mark unclear | Keep |
| Ranges/preferred | Required minimum; any number; warn on preferred | Required minimum only |

**User's choice:** If maximum is 3, remove jobs requiring more than 3 years. Ignore title seniority and preferred-only years; keep ambiguous jobs.
**Notes:** A 3–5 required range counts as minimum 3 and remains eligible at a setting of 3.
