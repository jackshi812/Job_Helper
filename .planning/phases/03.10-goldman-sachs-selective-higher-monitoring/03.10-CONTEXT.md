<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Exact Company and URL Identity
- **D-01:** Target exactly Goldman Sachs through the existing server-owned
  `goldman_higher:roles` identity and anonymous structured Higher GraphQL
  contract. Caller-controlled hosts, GraphQL paths, operations, filters, or
  Oracle coordinates remain prohibited.
- **D-02:** Use `https://higher.gs.com/results` as the canonical Watchlist
  navigation URL. Accept exact `/results` and legacy `/roles`, with only a
  harmless trailing slash normalized; no other Higher path is an alias.
- **D-03:** Persist each job's provider-supplied HTTPS Goldman Oracle
  application URL as the Apply destination. Validate the exact reviewed Oracle
  host, Candidate Experience path, and external job ID before persistence.
- **D-04:** An unreviewed navigation path, redirect, host, GraphQL coordinate,
  or application host is provider drift. Fail closed, retain existing jobs,
  report Degraded with a bounded reason, and authorize no missing-job closure.

### Seven-Day Posting Window
- **D-05:** Admit only roles whose trustworthy provider-owned posting timestamp
  is no older than a rolling 168 hours at poll time. Validate the timestamp
  independently even if Goldman supplies a source-side recency filter.
- **D-06:** A missing, malformed, future, stale, or otherwise untrustworthy
  posting timestamp excludes that role and makes the observation incomplete.
  Unknown freshness is never admitted.
- **D-07:** When an already-ingested Goldman role ages beyond 168 hours while
  still provider-open, keep its provider lifecycle truthful but remove it from
  the current Goldman feed. Do not manufacture a closed status from age or
  selective absence.

### Provider-Owned Category Boundary
- **D-08:** Retain the exact Phase 03.8 allowed terms: **Data**,
  **Technology**, **Finance**, **Investment**, **Research**, **Risk**, and
  **Capital Markets**.
- **D-09:** Either trusted provider field, `jobFunction` or `division`, may
  supply positive category evidence. One positive field is sufficient when the
  other is empty; titles and descriptions never substitute for provider
  evidence.
- **D-10:** Require the positive category term and provider label to agree
  between list and detail evidence. Conflicting or disappearing evidence makes
  the observation incomplete.
- **D-11:** Match terms case-insensitively using normalized whole words and
  whole phrases. `Capital Markets Technology` qualifies; `Database
  Administration` does not match `Data`; substring matching is prohibited.

### Career Population and Preference Handoff
- **D-12:** Request both Goldman **Early Career** and **Professional**
  populations.
- **D-13:** Add no Goldman-specific title, seniority, analyst/associate,
  manager, or experience heuristic. After source, country, recency, category,
  and completeness checks, the existing preference and title-filter pipeline
  remains the sole relevance boundary.
- **D-14:** A role proven outside the requested Early Career/Professional
  populations is excluded and makes the observation incomplete rather than
  silently widening scope.
- **D-15:** Internships and temporary early-career programs may pass the
  connector boundary when they satisfy the same exact U.S., 168-hour, and
  category evidence. Existing user preferences decide whether they appear as
  relevant matches.

### Completeness, Activation, and Acceptance
- **D-16:** Reconcile the complete bounded Goldman listing population with
  stable role/source IDs, exact expected counts, every page, deduplication, and
  full detail evidence for every selected job. Zero-result, capped, timed-out,
  count-drifted, schema-drifted, or partially hydrated observations cannot
  activate or authorize closure.
- **D-17:** Every Goldman selective observation sets
  `allowMissingClosure=false`. Failed or incomplete observations retain
  last-known jobs and health history; rolling-window absence never closes a
  job.
- **D-18:** Reuse the existing Experimental-to-Active contract: exact live
  terminal proof, three clean distinct server-timed positive observations,
  replay/same-window rejection, then one healthy natural scheduled poll on the
  exact deployed release.
- **D-19:** Successful completion requires at least one real persisted Goldman
  role with exact source, U.S., 168-hour, category, full-description, and
  working Apply-link evidence. If the live contract cannot prove this, Goldman
  remains precise Unsupported with no operational authority.
- **D-20:** Production migration/function deployment, live observations,
  activation, and UAT require fresh exact-release approvals. Final UAT occurs
  only in the owner's existing signed-in browser; Codex must not open or
  control a browser.
- **D-21:** Goldman is isolated from JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, Charles Schwab,
  every active Workday source, public ATS sources, and real user data. The
  rollout must prove protected identities, activation, job ownership, and
  evidence unchanged except ordinary scheduler-owned timestamps, with zero
  verifier residue.

### the agent's Discretion
- Choose the exact Higher GraphQL posting-date and experience evidence fields
  after current live-contract research, provided D-05 through D-07 and D-12
  through D-15 remain directly provable rather than inferred.
- Choose pagination/detail bounds, concurrency, time reserve, scheduling
  stagger, evidence digests, migration numbering, and finite verifier
  mechanics without weakening D-16 through D-21.
- Choose the safest existing-schema-compatible mechanism that removes
  over-seven-day Goldman roles from the current feed without representing them
  as provider-closed.

</decisions>
