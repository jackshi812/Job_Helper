## Decisions Made

- The SQL page returns `row_data`, `cursor_data`, and repeated `has_more`; the extra continuation row is consumed internally and never appears in results.
- A deterministic 64-bit query signature detects client filter drift. It is not an authorization token: RLS and the RPC predicates independently constrain every returned row.
- Company options apply lifecycle and tier scope over the complete authorized result set but deliberately ignore hidden-company selection, preserving re-selection.
