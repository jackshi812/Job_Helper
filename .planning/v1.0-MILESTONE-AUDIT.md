## Non-Blocking Warning

The active/dismissed `dashboard_feed_page` RPC has no Watchlist-source
parameter. The client fetches the combined 200-row page and then removes
external rows. If 200 newer external rows precede a watched row, Watchlist
Jobs can say “No watchlist matches yet” until Load More reaches a later page.
Applied history avoids this through the server-projected
`has_watched_company` field.

   page totals and empty states are truthful.
