Proven via the integration harness only; no deploy.**

## Accomplishments
- `detect.ts`: widened the workday `DetectResult` variant to `{ slug, region, site, hostForm }` (registry-validated, not free strings). Generalized Form A to an anchored `^([a-z0-9-]+)\.(wd\d{1,3})\.myworkdayjobs\.com$` host match, added a Form B branch with an anchored `^(wd\d{1,3})\.myworkdaysite\.com$` host match parsing `/{locale}/recruiting/{tenant}/{site}` (4 segments, literal `recruiting` at index 1, strictSlug tenant+site, reject `url.search`). Both branches gate final admission through `resolveWorkdayIdentity(...)` — unadmitted tuples fall through to `unsupported`. `buildEndpoint` derives `${identity.cxsRoot}/jobs` from the resolved identity; `UNSUPPORTED_URL_MESSAGE` generalized to "allowlisted Workday boards".
- `connectors.ts`: added `workdayIdentityForDetection` (resolves from the detected tuple, throws `invalid_identity`) and `workdayIdentityForCompany` (reconstructs from `company.source_key` via `WORKDAY_IDENTITIES`). `canonicalCareersUrl`, `deterministicSourceKey`, and `verifyWorkday` now identity-derive (Capital One byte-identical). `providerRegistry.workday.poll` reconstructs the identity from the persisted source key and calls `pollWorkdayRecent(identity, fetch, { knownIds })`. The `pollConnector` workday re-check became a registry allowlist match (`board_token/region/site` vs the resolved tuple; fail-closed `inactive_connector:workday_identity_not_allowed`). Region types widened to `string | null` on `PollConnectorCompany` and `ConnectorVerification`.
- `verify-board/index.ts`: `sourceKeyForDetection` derives the Workday source key from the resolved identity (fallback to the deterministic `workday:{region}:{tenant}:{site}`); swapped the `CAPITAL_ONE_WORKDAY_SOURCE_KEY` import for `resolveWorkdayIdentity`. Auth (Bearer + `role==='authenticated'`), SSRF/`redirect:'error'` boundary, staged-activation insert, and RPC observation path unchanged.
- `poll-tick/index.ts`: `Company.region` widened to `string | null` (the fourth region-check site called out in `.continue-here.md`), so Fidelity's `wd1` is no longer silently narrowed out.
- Tests: `detect.test.ts` gained the Form B positive, the Capital One `hostForm:'jobs'` regression, all Form B negatives (lookalike host, missing/extra segment, missing `recruiting` literal, search/hash/http/creds/port, encoded slash, unadmitted tenant + region) and the Fidelity `buildEndpoint` case. `workday-connector.integration.test.ts` gained a full verify-board handler test (Fidelity Form B → sourceKey `workday:wd1:fmr:FidelityCareers` + jobCount + `activation_state:'experimental'` insert) and a non-allowlisted-tenant rejection at verify (`invalid_identity`) and poll (`inactive_connector:workday_identity_not_allowed`).

## Decisions Made
- **Form A generalized rather than left literal:** the plan's D-01 admits both shapes generically, so Form A parses `{tenant}.{region}.myworkdayjobs.com` with an anchored regex and defers admission to `resolveWorkdayIdentity`. Every pre-existing negative (wrong region wd11, other tenant, other site, `.evil.example` lookalike, http/creds/port, `Capital%2FOne`) stays rejected, and Capital One resolves byte-identical — verified by the untouched Capital One integration cases.
- **hostForm is a required field on the DetectResult:** because the source-key tuple alone cannot disambiguate Form A from Form B (A1), the disambiguator is carried on the detect result. This forces exact-match (`toEqual`) detect assertions to include `hostForm`; the Capital One assertions were updated to `hostForm:'jobs'` per the plan's behavior block.
- **Fidelity company name:** `identity.companyName` is `null` for Fidelity (no live-probe name this phase), so `verifyWorkday` falls back to the tenant slug `fmr` for the staged company name. Downstream watchlist presentation (Plan 03) keys the Fidelity filter note on the source key, not the name.
